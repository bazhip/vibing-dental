Data Processing Addendum

Effective: November 12, 2026

This Data Processing Addendum (“DPA”) forms part of the Terms of Service between ToothOps Charting, operated by Timothy O’Brien (“ToothOps”, “we”, “us”), and the practice or individual using ToothOps (“you”, the “Customer”). It applies automatically when you accept the Terms, with no separate signature needed, and covers personal data that ToothOps processes on your behalf when it is subject to the EU General Data Protection Regulation (“GDPR”), the UK GDPR and Data Protection Act 2018, or the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (together, “Data Protection Law”). Terms such as “controller”, “processor”, “personal data”, “data subject” and “personal data breach” have the meanings given in Data Protection Law. If you need a countersigned copy for your records, email bazhip@gmail.com.

1. Roles and scope

You are the controller, and ToothOps is your processor, of the personal data contained in the chart content, images and other material you or your team put into ToothOps (“Customer Personal Data”). Details of the processing are in Annex 1. ToothOps is a controller of account, billing and usage information, which is covered by our Privacy Policy, not this DPA.

2. Your instructions

We process Customer Personal Data only on your documented instructions. The Terms, this DPA, and your use and configuration of ToothOps are your complete instructions. The exception is where the law requires otherwise; in that case we’ll tell you first unless the law prohibits it. We’ll tell you if we believe an instruction breaks Data Protection Law. You are responsible for having a lawful basis for the Customer Personal Data you enter, and for giving any notices your clients need.

3. Confidentiality

Anyone we authorise to process Customer Personal Data is bound by confidentiality obligations.

4. Security

We maintain appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect Customer Personal Data, described in Annex 2. We may update these measures as long as the overall level of protection doesn’t decrease.

5. Sub-processors

You give general authorisation for us to engage sub-processors. The current list is in the “Who we share information with” section of our Privacy Policy. Before adding or replacing a sub-processor, we’ll update that list and notify account owners by email or in the app at least 14 days in advance. You can object on reasonable data protection grounds within that period. If we can’t reasonably accommodate the objection, you may stop using the affected feature or close your account. We impose data protection obligations on each sub-processor that are substantially as protective as this DPA, and we remain responsible for their performance.

6. Helping you meet your obligations

  • Data subject requests — ToothOps lets you view, correct, export (as PDF) and delete chart content yourself. If you can’t handle a request in the app, we’ll help within a reasonable time. If a data subject contacts us directly about Customer Personal Data, we’ll refer them to you.
  • Assessments — we’ll give you reasonable information you need for a data protection impact assessment or a consultation with a supervisory authority about ToothOps.

7. Personal data breaches

We’ll notify you without undue delay, and where feasible within 48 hours, after becoming aware of a personal data breach affecting Customer Personal Data. We’ll include the information you reasonably need to meet your own notification obligations, as it becomes available, and take reasonable steps to contain and remedy the breach.

8. Deletion at the end of the service

You can download your charts as PDFs at any time. When you delete chart content, your account or your practice, we delete the related Customer Personal Data from the live service as described in our Privacy Policy. Residual copies in backups are overwritten in the normal backup cycle. The exception is anything the law requires us to keep.

9. Audits

We’ll make available the information reasonably needed to demonstrate compliance with this DPA and answer your reasonable written questions. If that isn’t enough, or a supervisory authority requires it, you may audit our compliance, including through an independent auditor bound by confidentiality. You must give at least 30 days’ notice, audits are limited to once a year, and they must be carried out at your cost during normal business hours. Our sub-processors’ own certifications and audit reports may be used to address their part of the processing.

10. International transfers

ToothOps is operated from, and hosts Customer Personal Data in, the United States. To the extent your transfer of Customer Personal Data to ToothOps is a restricted transfer under Data Protection Law, the following apply, incorporated into this DPA by reference:

  • EEA: the Standard Contractual Clauses approved by European Commission Implementing Decision (EU) 2021/914, Module Two (controller to processor), with you as data exporter and ToothOps as data importer. The optional Clause 7 does not apply. Under Clause 9(a), Option 2 (general authorisation) applies, with the notice period in section 5. The optional language in Clause 11 does not apply. For Clauses 17 and 18, the governing law and courts are those of Ireland. Annexes I and II are completed by Annexes 1 and 2 of this DPA. The competent supervisory authority under Clause 13 is determined by the exporter’s establishment or representative.
  • UK: the International Data Transfer Addendum to the EU Standard Contractual Clauses issued by the UK Information Commissioner (version B1.0), with the tables completed from this DPA. Either party may end it as allowed by its Section 19.
  • Switzerland: the EU Standard Contractual Clauses as above, with references to the GDPR read as references to the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection, and the Swiss Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner as the competent supervisory authority where the FADP applies.

If the Standard Contractual Clauses conflict with this DPA or the Terms, the Clauses prevail.

11. General

This DPA lasts as long as we process Customer Personal Data for you. Each party’s liability under this DPA is subject to the limitations in the Terms, except where Data Protection Law or the Standard Contractual Clauses do not allow it. On the processing of personal data, this DPA prevails over the Terms. We may update this DPA to reflect changes in law or in the service, but we won’t reduce the protection it gives Customer Personal Data. We’ll update the date above and notify account owners of material changes.

Annex 1 — Details of processing

PartiesData exporter / controller: the Customer (identified by its ToothOps account and practice details). Data importer / processor: Timothy O’Brien, operating ToothOps Charting, Colorado, USA — bazhip@gmail.com.
Subject matter and purposeProviding ToothOps veterinary dental charting: storing, syncing and displaying charts and images, team sharing, PDF and owner-report generation, optional voice/AI autofill, and sending the emails the Customer requests (for example recheck reminders to pet owners).
Nature of processingCollection, storage, organisation, retrieval, transmission to sub-processors, display, export and deletion.
Data subjectsThe Customer’s clients (pet owners), and the Customer’s staff whose names appear in chart content.
Categories of personal dataPet owner names, phone numbers and email addresses; staff names (for example tech or doctor); free-text notes the Customer chooses to enter; images and radiographs (which may include incidental personal data); voice dictation, transcribed but not stored as audio.
Special category dataNone intended. Records concern animals; the Customer should not enter special category data about people.
FrequencyContinuous, while the Customer uses ToothOps.
Duration and retentionFor as long as the Customer keeps the content in ToothOps, then deleted as described in section 8.
Sub-processorsAs listed in the Privacy Policy. Customer Personal Data is processed only as needed for each provider’s function (for example storage, email delivery, transcription or AI autofill).

Annex 2 — Security measures

  • Encryption: all traffic uses TLS. Our database and file storage provider encrypts data at rest.
  • Access control: database row-level security restricts each record to its account and practice. Storage for photos and radiographs is private, served only through short-lived signed links. Administrative access is limited to the operator, enforced server-side, and every administrative chart view is logged.
  • Authentication: passwords are stored only as secure hashes by our authentication provider. Sign in with Google is also offered.
  • Least privilege: privileged credentials are held only in server-side functions, never in the browser.
  • Resilience: the chart in progress is also kept on the user’s device, so a dropped connection doesn’t lose work, and any chart can be exported as a PDF at any time.
  • Vendor management: sub-processors are reputable providers bound by their own data processing agreements.
  • Data minimisation: voice audio is streamed for transcription and not stored. We don’t use Customer Personal Data for advertising or sell it.
