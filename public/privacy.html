Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 12, 2026

ToothOps Charting (“ToothOps”, “we”, “us”) is veterinary dental charting software available at toothops.app. ToothOps is operated by Timothy O’Brien, an individual based in Colorado, USA. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, who we share it with, and the choices you have.

Questions? Email bazhip@gmail.com.

Who this applies to

ToothOps is a tool for veterinary professionals and their teams. It applies to people who create an account, people invited to a practice, and anyone who uses the free no-account trial. If you enter information about your clients (pet owners) into ToothOps, you are responsible for having the right to do so; we process that information on your behalf to provide the service.

Our role

For your account, billing, and usage information, ToothOps is the controller — we decide how that information is used, as described in this policy. For the chart content your practice enters — including patient records and any pet owner contact details — your practice is the controller and ToothOps acts as its processor: we handle that information only on your practice’s instructions, to provide the service. Our Data Processing Addendum sets out those terms and forms part of our Terms of Service.

Information we collect

  • Account information — your email address and password (stored only as a secure hash), or, if you sign in with Google, the name, email address and profile picture link Google shares with us. Your practice name, doctor name and an optional practice logo.
  • Chart content you enter — patient (animal) details, exam findings, measurements, diagrams, notes, treatment reports, anesthesia records, and recheck dates. Charts may include pet owner contact details (name, phone, email) that you choose to record.
  • Photos and radiographs you upload, including DICOM files.
  • Voice dictation — if you use voice input, your audio is streamed for transcription while you dictate. We don’t store recordings.
  • Team and billing information — practice membership and roles, subscription plan and status. Card details are handled by Stripe; we never see or store your full card number.
  • Usage and device information — basic, privacy-friendly analytics (pages viewed, approximate location from IP address, browser type), AI usage counts for the features you use, and error reports you choose to send.
  • Information stored on your device — the chart you’re working on is saved in your browser’s local storage so nothing is lost on refresh. We don’t use advertising cookies.

How we use information

  • To provide ToothOps: sign you in, save and sync your charts, generate PDFs and owner reports, share charts with your practice team, and send the emails you ask for (for example invitations, password resets, and recheck reminders to pet owners).
  • To power optional AI features: when you use voice or AI autofill, the relevant chart text or dictation is sent to our AI and transcription providers to fill in the chart. Their output is a suggestion for you to review.
  • To run billing for paid plans.
  • To keep the service secure, fix problems, and understand which features are used so we can improve them.
  • To contact you about your account or important changes to the service.

We do not sell your information, and we do not use your chart content for advertising.

International transfers

ToothOps is operated from the United States, and our database and file storage are hosted in the United States (Supabase, US East region). Our providers may also process information in other countries. If you’re in the EEA, the UK or Switzerland, this means your information is transferred to countries that may not have the same data protection laws as yours. Where we or our providers transfer personal information out of those regions, we rely on the European Commission’s Standard Contractual Clauses (with the UK Addendum or Swiss amendments where applicable) included in our providers’ data processing agreements, or on a provider’s certification under the EU–U.S. Data Privacy Framework and its UK and Swiss extensions. For chart content we process on a practice’s behalf, the transfer terms in our Data Processing Addendum apply. You can email us for more information about these safeguards.

Google user data

If you sign in with Google, we receive only your name, email address and profile picture link (the “email” and “profile” permissions). We use them solely to create and sign in to your ToothOps account and to show your name on your profile. We do not access your Gmail, contacts, calendar, or other Google data, and we do not share Google user data with third parties except the service providers above as needed to run your account. ToothOps’ use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

How long we keep information

We keep your account and charts for as long as your account is active. You can delete individual charts at any time. When you delete your account, we delete your account, your private charts, and their photos and radiographs. Charts you shared with a practice stay with that practice. When a practice owner deletes a practice, its shared charts and images are deleted. Unclaimed team invitations are removed after they expire. Copies may persist briefly in backups before being overwritten.

Your choices and rights

  • Access and correct — view and edit your charts and profile in the app.
  • Delete — delete charts in the app, or delete your whole account from Account settings.
  • Export — download any chart as a PDF.

Depending on where you live, you may have more rights over your personal information. If you’re in the EEA, the UK or Switzerland, you have the right to:

  • Access — get a copy of the personal information we hold about you.
  • Rectification — have inaccurate information corrected.
  • Erasure — have your information deleted.
  • Restriction — ask us to limit how we use your information.
  • Portability — receive information you gave us in a structured, machine-readable format.
  • Objection — object to processing based on our legitimate interests.
  • Withdraw consent — where we rely on consent, withdraw it at any time (this doesn’t affect processing before you withdrew it).
  • Complain — lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority (in the UK, the Information Commissioner’s Office). We’d appreciate the chance to address your concern first.

To exercise any of these rights, email bazhip@gmail.com. We’ll respond within one month and may need to verify your identity first. We won’t treat you differently for exercising your rights.

Pet owners whose details were entered by a veterinary practice should contact that practice first; we’ll assist the practice with requests.

Security

Information is encrypted in transit, access to data is restricted per account and practice, and storage for photos and radiographs is private. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong password and keep your sign-in details to yourself.

Children

ToothOps is a professional tool and is not directed to children under 16. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as ToothOps changes. We’ll update the date above, and for significant changes we’ll notify account holders by email or in the app.
